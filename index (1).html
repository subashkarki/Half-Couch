For your Ultra + AirPods with the phone locked in your belt. Set this up in Apple Workout before starting. This website cannot send the workout to your Watch.

  1. On Apple Watch, open Workout → Outdoor Run → workout options → Custom. Labels vary by watchOS version. On supported iOS versions, you can also build it in iPhone Fitness.
  2. Add these time or distance steps in order. For repeated work/recovery pairs, use Repeat. Include the final recovery.

    Choose workout views showing elapsed time, distance, pace and heart rate. Use the Up Next view for custom intervals. In Watch Settings → Workout, enable Voice Feedback and connect your AirPods to the device providing the coaching.

    Test one short workout first. Apple’s alerts are not our custom spoken script. Use one coaching source at a time so voices do not overlap. The web timer and Watch do not synchronise pauses or completion.
